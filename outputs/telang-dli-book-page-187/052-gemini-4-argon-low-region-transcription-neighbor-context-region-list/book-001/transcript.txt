    stanza_note 103 'St. CIII' [u2]
      r3: St. CIII.—मनोरथोपरचितप्रासाद is equivalent to castle in the air. The Stanza occurs at p. 301 of the Kâvyasangraha.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 104 'St. CIV' [u3]
      r4: St. CIV.—आघ्रान is apparently used as a synonyme of ग्रस्त occurring in the fourth line. I observe that Râmarshi expressly says ग्रस्तमित्यर्थः. The word is used in a similar sense in the Das'akumâracharita p. 11 (Calc. Ed. 1925 Sam.) विद्युच्चलं यौवनम् it will be seen breaks the symmetry of the Stanza, but none of the variants preserve it. उपहता is a better reading than अपहृता. वा इति आश्चर्ये says Râmarshi. But see our note to Nîtis'ataka St. 32.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 105 'St. CV' [u4]
      r5: St. CV.—आधिव्याधि may be taken as a Dvandva. In the MâlatîMâdhava (p. 145) where the expression occurs it is explained by Jagaddhara as आधय एव व्याधयः; unnecessarily, I think. Construe यत्र लक्ष्मीस्तत्र आपदः पतन्ति—आपद् is not a very usual form but occurs in Mṛichchhakaṭika p. 213 (Calc. Ed.) आत्मसात्. See Siddh. Kaum. I., 731. तत्किं नाम &c. “and what indeed is there which unbridled destiny has created well-constituted.”—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 106 'St. CVI' (open end) [u5]
      r6: St. CVI.—नियमित = cramped, compressed. The second line as it stands in our text is explained by both commentators, but it certainly seems to involve a pleonasm. “In youth which is painful through separation there is separation.” The reading of Bo. and N. would seem to be better. “The enjoyments of youth are painful by reason of the grief of separation.” नारीणामप्यवज्ञा is taken by the commentator with the second line and to this construction the two अपि would seem to give support. Râmarshi's reading is somewhat different and he takes the whole line as one construing असाधुः with अवज्ञा. This I think is better. See St. 111. As it stands the first अपि may be taken as meaning again, and इत्यतः or some expression of like import must be supplied after विलसति.

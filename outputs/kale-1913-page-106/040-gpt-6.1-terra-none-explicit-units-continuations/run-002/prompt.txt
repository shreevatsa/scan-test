Inspect this scanned book page and divide it into regions, one per text unit, for cropping and reassembly.

Return JSON only. Use bounding boxes [x1,y1,x2,y2] normalized to 0..1000,
with the origin at the top-left. Give each region a type, a keep flag, and
reading_order among content regions. Furniture regions may have null order.

Make each region exactly one unit: the finest unit that the page sets apart visually. Types:
- verse: one verse or stanza, with all its lines and its number or end mark (for example ॥ १२ ॥). Never one region per line, and never two verses in one region, even when consecutive verses are set with no space between them. If other material interrupts a verse, each printed part is its own region.
- paragraph: one prose paragraph, including one continued from the previous page or onto the next. Prose translations and commentary are paragraphs.
- heading: one heading, title, or speaker line.
- lead_in: one short line that introduces a following verse, quotation or display, set on a line of its own and often ending in a dash or colon.
- list_item: one entry of a list, table of contents or index.
- formula: one displayed formula, equation or list of values set apart from the prose. Lines that continue one another (for example a chain of = or ≡) form one formula; displays separated by prose or by space are separate.
- note: one footnote, endnote or marginal note, or one entry of an apparatus of readings, including its marker. Separate neighboring notes even when they share a printed line. A paragraph or verse set apart within the notes area is its own region.
- table: one table, including any rotated text in it.
- figure, caption: an illustration and its caption, as separate regions.
- running_header, page_number: page furniture, keep=false.
- other: anything else. Printer's signature marks and catchwords are other, keep=false.

- Include every visible glyph of the unit, with its diacritics, marks above and below the line, and punctuation. Add modest padding without swallowing a neighboring unit.
- Ignore scanner borders, digitization watermarks, speckles, decorative frames, ornaments and rules.
- Continuation: give each region two flags.
  continues_from_earlier is true if the region does not begin its unit: the unit began in an earlier region, on a previous page, in a previous column, or before something that interrupts it. Examples: a paragraph that starts mid-sentence without indentation at the top of a page or column; the second half of a verse after an interrupting translation; a note continued from the previous page's notes.
  continues_later is true if the unit does not end in this region: it goes on in a later region, on the next page or column, or after an interruption. Examples: a paragraph whose last line ends mid-sentence or with a hyphenated word at the bottom of a page or column; a verse that has not reached its number or end mark.
  Both are false for a unit that is complete in its region, and for page furniture.
- Number content regions in the order a reader reads them. On a page with columns, finish each column from top to bottom before starting the next, unless the layout clearly says otherwise.
- Flag uncertain grouping with uncertain=true instead of silently dropping or merging material.
- Do not transcribe, translate, or regenerate the text. Only propose regions.

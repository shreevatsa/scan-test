- heading (printed as regions): A title or heading: a part's title, such as CONTENTS., TRANSLATION or NOTES.; a śataka's title, such as अथ श्रीमद्भर्तृहरिविरचितं above नीतिशतकम् ।; an invocation above it, such as ॥ श्रीः ॥ or ॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥; a topic heading inside a śataka, such as अथ तृष्णादूषणम् ।; or a heading for the miscellaneous stanzas.

- commentator_verse (printed as regions): One of the commentator's own opening verses at the start of the Nītiśataka, in the commentary's type, before his introduction; each ends with ॥.
- introduction (printed as regions): The commentator's introduction at the start of a śataka, in the commentary's type, after his opening verses and before the first verse of Bhartṛhari.

- lead_in (printed as regions): A short line of its own that introduces the verse.
- verse (printed as regions): One verse in large type, with its number, such as ॥ २६ ॥.
- commentary (printed as regions): The Sanskrit commentary on the verse, in smaller type. It usually ends with the name of the metre, such as शिखरिणी ॥.
- translated_stanza (printed as regions): The English translation of one stanza: a paragraph beginning with the stanza's number, such as 4. or I.; some are only a cross-reference, such as 1 See p. 16.
- stanza_note (printed as regions): The English note on one stanza, from its number, such as Sl. 5., to the next note. A note can have several paragraphs, each its own region, and can run across pages.
- footnote (printed as regions; may stand at the top of flow notes): One footnote below the rule: in the Sanskrit text, a numbered variant reading; in the English parts, a note marked * or †.

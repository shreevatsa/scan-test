    stanza_note (open start) [u2]
      r3: than °नाः क्लेशान्. नियमितप्राण—Râmarshi says प्राणाः श्वासा इन्द्रियाणि वा for the latter of which no authority is given. The former meaning is quite appropriate. Comp. Vikramorvashî opening Stanza and Raghuvans'a VIII., 19. The last line may be thus rendered “we have performed exactly those various actions which are performed by the sages, but we have not obtained their various fruits.” Râmarshi renders वञ्चिताः by रहिताः adding तत्तपःफलम् न अलभामहि. Rightly; compare Naishadha I., 127 and Raghu VII., 8 with Mallinâtha’s comment. This Stanza occurs also at p. 302 of the Kâvyasangraha.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 14 'St. XIV.' [u3]
      r4: St. XIV.—तृष्णा तरुणायते. Comp. St. 12 and Mohamudgara St. 15. (Kâvyasangraha p. 33).—Anushṭub.
    stanza_note 15 'St. XV.' [u4]
      r5: St. XV.—अम्बर = either sky or cloth. There is an equivoque here. दौर्गत्य comp. दुर्गत. Nîtis'ataka St. 56.—Anushṭub.
    stanza_note 16 'St. XVI.' [u5]
      r6: St. XVI.—वियोग seems to answer to स्वयं त्यक्ता in the fourth line meaning voluntary separation. The commentary introduces the third line thus ननु गमनशीलत्वात्स्वयमेव यास्यन्ति को लाभस्तत्त्यागे इत्याशङ्क्याह ॥ अतुलपरितापाय. For the dative see Siddh. Kaum. I., 280. For the last line comp. Gîtâ chap. II., 70 et seq. The Stanza occurs at p. 311 of the Kâvyasangraha.—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 17 'St. XVII.' [u6]
      r7: St. XVII.—व्याकोश is given by Amara as a synonyme of प्रफुल्ल, विकच, &c. The first line may then be thus translated : “Desire is allayed when self-restraint developed by means of true discrimination opens up [in a man].” शम is said in the Vedântasâra to mean श्रवणादि (i. e. श्रवणमनननिदिध्यासन) व्यतिरिक्तविषयेभ्यो मनसो निग्रहः. परिष्बङ्ग = contact with objects of sense (स्रक्चन्दनवनितादि as the Vedântins say) परिणति = result, end. The third line is not quite clear. It seems to mean “wretched by reason of the strong distraction of the enjoyment of greatness old through age”. यस्याम् of course is correlative to सा.—S'ikhariṇî.

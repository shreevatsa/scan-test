      r3: 6. For the sake of this life (lit. these vital airs) that is compared to water on a lotus leaf, what, indeed, have we, destitute of discernment, not done ?—since in the presence of the rich whose minds are blinded by the pride of wealth, we, shameless that we are, have committed the sin of even mentioning (singing the praises of) our own virtues.
    translated_stanza 7 'Stanza 7' [u4]
      r4: 7 We forbore but not through forgiveness; we relinquished domestic happiness—but not willingly; we endured the pain caused by inclement cold, wind and sun—but did not (thereby) practise austerities; day and night did we think of weath—but not, with restrained vital airs, of the feet of S'iva. We did the various actions which ascetics perform; but as to the several fruits ( obtained by them ) we have been deceived.
    translated_stanza 8 'Stanza 8' [u5]

r2Very few points now remain to be discussed. Was Bhartṛihari—the author of the S'atakas—the same as the Bhartṛihari or Hari who is known as the author of the Vâkyapadîya? We have already by implication answered this question in the affirmative on the authority of tradition. That tradition is alluded to by Colebrooke* who does not question it; and the same remark applies to Bohlen.† Was Bhartṛihari identical with the author of the Bhaṭṭikâvya? For an affirmative answer to this question, there is no better or stronger ground than the identity of name,‡ but even this ground is exceedingly weak. Thus in the commentary of Bharata on Bhaṭṭi, the name of the author is given as Bhartṛihari, but nothing more is said about him.

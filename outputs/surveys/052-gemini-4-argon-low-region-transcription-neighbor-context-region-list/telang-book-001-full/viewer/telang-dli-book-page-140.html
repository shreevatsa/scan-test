stanza_note 37 “St. XXXVII.”
r4St. XXXVII.—यत्—तत् = since or seeing that—then. इन and भर्तृ both are names of the Sun. III. Amarakosh, Nânârtha. विकृति in nearly the same sense as निकृति which itself occurs as a variant here in some copies. It means something that is wrong, offence, injury. For विकृति comp. Uttararâmacharita 134 तदपि खलु मे स व्याहारस्तुरङ्गमराक्षिणां ॥ विकृतिमखिलक्षत्राक्षेपप्रचण्डतयाकरोत । For निकृति compare Kirâta I., 43 or III., 44. There is a double meaning in पाद, foot or ray. For the construction सहते standing for सहेत comp. Vîracharita pp. 75-24. Compare for the idea Uttararâmacharita p. 148. मयूखैरश्रान्तं तपति यदि देवो दिनकरः ॥ किमाग्नेयग्रावा निकृत इव तेजांसि वमति. —Âryâ.
stanza_note 38 “St. XXXVIII.”
r5St. XXXVIII.—About the meaning of कपोलभित्ति see Mallinâtha's commentary on Raghu. V., 43. For प्रकृति comp. Stanza 52. The idea of the last line finds frequent expression in Sanskṛit Literature ; तेजसा सह जातानां वयः कुत्रोपयुज्यते or Kâlidâsa's तेजसां हि न वयः समीक्ष्यते, or Bhavabhûti's गुणाः पूजास्थानं गुणिषु न च लिङ्गं न च वयः are other well-known instances.—Âryâ.
stanza_note 39 “St. XXXIX.”
r6St. XXXIX.—गच्छताम् which has been stated to be the reading of most of the copies consulted is difficult of explanation grammatically, गम् by itself not being given in the grammars or used in the literature as an Âtmaṇepadi root. अभिजन the commentator renders by स्वजन, but as all the other things enumerated are qualities residing in an individual, that meaning, even if allowable elsewhere, is not admissible here. Here it means nobility of birth as in S'âkuntala अभिजनवतो भर्तुः श्लाघ्ये स्थिता गृहिणीपदे and many places besides. वैरिणि = अनुपकारिणि says Râmarshi. The positive meaning, however, is not bad.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

stanza_note 89
r3St. LXXXIX.—वादे &c. and विनीतोचिता are taken together by the commentators. But there does seem to be some little incompatibility between the two. I would suggest that they be taken separately as designating two different descriptions of विद्या corresponding to the two uses mentioned in the familiar Stanza विद्या विवादाय धनं मदाय &c. Râmarshi takes अधररस as equivalent to अधर एव रसः; wrongly, I think; अधरस्य रसः is more usual and better.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

stanza_note 91
r5St. XCI.—जीर्ण=वृद्ध. (Râmarshi) but it means absorbed as in St. 2. For the second line also comp. St. 2. हा is better than ह्य़ा in the fourth line. With ह्या, आ might be taken as standing by itself, it being the आ which is स्मरणे. A curious variant for वन्ध्य is विन्ध्य which Râmarshi comments on, though in the copy we have, the text is written वन्ध्य. He says विन्ध्याचलफलतुल्यत्वं याताः प्राप्ताः। विन्ध्याचलस्य हि अतिगहनत्वेन मनुष्याणां दुःसंचारत्वात् अनेकानि फलानि अनुपभोग्यत्वान्निरर्थकानि भवन्ति.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

r6St. XCII.—वलित=मिश्रित (Râmarshi.) Comp. Mâlatî-Mâdhava अमृतसंवलितैरिवाङ्गैः (p. 152). People misunderstand the mere cure of pain as itself a pleasure.—S'ikhariṇî.
stanza_note 93
r7St. XCIII.—आत्मनि ब्रह्मणि त्वयि आसमन्ताद्भावेन रामः क्रीडा यस्य स तथा स्वात्मानुभवसंपन्न इत्यर्थः (Râmarshi). At Gîtâ V., 24 where the expression अन्तरारामः occurs. S'ankara explains it by अन्तरेवात्मन्यारामः क्रीडा यस्य सः. After giving the ordinary meaning of फलाशी Râmarshi says अथवा फले मोक्षलक्षणे आशा वाञ्छा विद्यते यस्य स तथा. On समकरचरणे which is Râmarshi's reading he writes समकरचरणो हि पुमान्धनवान्भवतीति सामुद्रिके प्रसिद्धम् and also मत्स्यांकितचरणो हि पुमान्धनवान्भवतीत्यपि the latter being required for the alternative meaning of मकरेण सहितौ चरणौ and not समौ करचरणौ यस्य.—Sragdharâ.

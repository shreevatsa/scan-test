stanza_note 79 “St. LXXIX.”
r3St. LXXIX.—हेमगिरि, रजताद्रि = Meru and Himâlaya (commentary.) मन्यामहे = esteem, respect; not a very usual employment of the root. मानय is commoner ; but मन् may be seen used in the form मन in this sense at Raghu. VIII., 8 and Mallinâth's commentary renders it by मन्यमानः. This Stanza is given in the Kâvyasangraha p. 40.—Vasantatilakâ.
stanza_note 80 “St. LXXX.”
r4St. LXXX.—Comp. St. 27. For the story see Râmâyaṇa referred to in the note to St. 35. See also Matsya Purâṇa CCI., 1-4 and 21-22.—Upajâti.
stanza_note 81 “St. LXXXI.”
r5St. LXXXI.—मनस्वी = strong-minded. कार्यार्थी = bent on business. —S'ikhariṇî.
stanza_note 82 “St. LXXXII.”
r6St. LXXXII.—सर्वेषाम् = of all persons, or of all conditions of life. This alternative meaning is suggested as the text has once ऐश्वर्य and at another time प्रभवितृ. सर्वकारण = cause of all i. e. सुजनता and such other “ornaments.” Râmarshi has ऐश्वर्यादीनाम् for both सर्व. शील = शुद्धचरित Amara Nâṭyavarga 26. See below St. CIX. and Misc. St. XIX.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 83 “St. LXXXIII.”
r7St. LXXXIII.—यदि वा has no conditional sense, it is equivalent to अथवा. For पदम् see Siddh. Kaum. I., 268. धियं बुद्धिमरीरयन्ति विचारेण सर्वतः प्रवर्तयन्ति ते धीराः Râmarshi.—Vasantatilakâ.
stanza_note 84 “St. LXXXIV.”
r8St. LXXXIV.—करण्ड is the box in which the snake charmer keeps his snakes. This Stanza inculcates fatalism pure and simple, and it is not easy to understand why this is included among the Stanzas of धैर्यप्रशंसा by the arrangers of the work.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 85 “St. LXXXV.”
r9St. LXXXV.—There is a play on the word वृत्त here. It means round as well as conduct, and साधुवृत्त as applied to the ball means well rounded, perfectly circular. Such a ball of course rebounds best. I see that Râmarshi gives the same explanation. —Anushṭub.

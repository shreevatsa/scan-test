stanza_note 18 “St. XVIII.”
r3St. XVIII.—For the second line compare Panchatantra (Bomb. Sans. Classics) I. p. 2 and Bhâminîvilâsa I., 12. The idea is very common in our poetry.—Vasantatilakâ.
stanza_note 19 “St. XIX.”
r4St. XIX.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 20 “St. XX.”
r5St. XX.—नाम is an idiomatic equivalent to “indeed,” and is in very common use. Compare Bhavabhûti's well known words ये नाम केचिदिह नः &c., or the equally well-known न नाम शरासनम् of the Vikramorvashî, and various other places प्रच्छन्नगुप्तम्=प्रच्छन्नं यथा स्यात्तथा गुप्तम्. Râmarshi has प्रच्छन्नं च तद्गुप्तं च. It means carefully protected. On गुरूणां गुरुः, the commentator has the following क्षुद्रगुरवः सर्वविद्यासंपूर्णप्राज्ञं प्रष्टुमायान्ति स प्राज्ञो गुरूणामपि गुरुः ॥ अथ च गृणाति हितमुपदिशति इति गुरुरिति गुरुपदव्याख्यानमतो हितकर्त्री विद्यैव. This is too far-fetched, and not free from difficulties. गुरूणां गुरुः is simply greatest of the great. गुरु is feminine (Pâṇini IV., 1, 44). For the genitive see Pâṇini II., 3, 41. राजसु is understood by the commentator as a Locative, which is probably right. But it is somewhat difficult to account for it, having regard to Pâṇini II., 3, 67. To suit this rule राजसुपूजिता might be taken as a compound सु going with पूजिता.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 21 “St. XXI.”
r6St. XXI.—The commentator says देहिनां प्राणिनां यदि क्षान्तिः क्षमा वर्तते तदा सान्त्वनवचनेन किं प्रयोजनम् तथा च क्रोधोस्ति तर्हि अरिभिः शत्रुभिः किम् ॥ ज्ञातिश्चेदनलेन किम् । दायादाः सहजारय इति वर्तते ॥ ज्ञातयो यदा निन्दादिपरुषं वदन्ति तेनैव दाहो भवति अग्नेः किं प्रयोजनम् ॥ .... यदि व्रीडा लज्जा असत्कर्मसु वर्तते तदा तदेव भूषणमितरभूषणैः किम् ॥ Compare Stanza 55 below. The meaning of the questions is—What more of good or evil (as the case may be) can accrue from the one than from the other? This Stanza also occurs at Kâvyasangraha p. 2.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 22 “St. XXII.”
r7St. XXII.—कला is art, in both senses of the word. “On them rests the world.” i. e. Without these qualities, society would be in disorder. Râmarshi renders लोके स्थितिः by जीवनमर्यादा. —S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

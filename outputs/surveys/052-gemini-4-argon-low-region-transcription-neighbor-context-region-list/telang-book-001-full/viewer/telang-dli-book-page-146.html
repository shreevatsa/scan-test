stanza_note 69 “St. LXIX”
r3St. LXIX.—ख्यापयन्तः. The commentator says स्वान्स्वकीयान्गुणान्परेषां गुणानां कथनैः सह ख्यापयन्तः कथयन्तः स्वान्गुणान्स्वयं न वदन्ति परेषां गुणानुवादाः क्रियन्ते तैरेव प्रसङ्गेन एतेषां गुणानुवर्णनं क्रियते इति कृत्वा स्वार्थान् &c. But this is scarcely correct, and it does not indicate any quality to be admired. The meaning is “showing their own good qualities (namely, good nature, freedom from envy &c.) by describing the good qualities of others.” आरम्भ = deed, act. Raghu. I., 15. साश्चर्यचर्या = of admirable conduct. Comp. Râjatarangiṇî p. 4 (Calc. Ed.) अथ साश्चर्यचर्यो भूसुरेन्द्रस्तत्सुतो नृपः—C. has वर्या for चर्या, and the commentator says आश्चर्येण सह वर्याः श्रेष्ठाः. But this is not satisfactory, and the other reading is much better.—Sragdharâ.

stanza_note 75 “St. LXXV”
r9St. LXXV.—उन्मनः = anxious, eager. The more common meaning is troubled with anxiety. युक्तम्—The commentator explains this by उचितम्; I think wrongly. It means ‘joined to’ पुनः does not make very good sense when taken with the words amongst which it stands, although it is not quite inexplicable there. It may be taken with युक्तंम् or शाम्यति. The Stanza it need scarcely be said gives a moral aspect to an actual physical phenomenon.—S'ârdûlavikrîdita

stanza_note 104 “St. CIV”
r3St. CIV.—The first line is ungrammatical, unless ह is taken as an expletive by itself. But ह is not used in the class of works to which these S'atakas belong. See St. 33.—Anushṭub.
stanza_note 105 “St. CV”
r4St. CV.—For दरिद्र comp. Kâvyaprakâs'a p. 60.—Âryâ.
stanza_note 106 “St. CVI”
r5St. CVI.—अधोमुखस्य कृतस्य is not a very usual construction. The reading of K. is better.—Upajâti.
stanza_note 107 “St. CVII”
r6St. CVII.—खनन्ति has been adopted instead of the दहन्ति of C. and the other copies, because, among other reasons, निर्दहति occurs immediately afterwards.—Vasantatilakâ.
stanza_note 108 “St. CVIII”
r7St. CVIII.—पाद = ray or foot. In the latter sense there would seem to be an allusion to the Vâmana incarnation of Vishṇu for which see Matsya Purâ ṇa CCXLVI., 66 et seq. स्फारस्फुरित. Comp. Vairâgyas'ataka St. 42, स्फारफुल्लत् occurs in Mâlatî-Mâdhava p. 181. स्फारं महत् Râmarshi.—Anushṭub.
stanza_note 109 “St. CIX”
r8St. CIX.—For the genitive तस्य, compare वीरो न यस्य भगवान्भृगुनन्दनोपि (Uttararâmacharita) माल्यगुण = flower-wreath. शीलं = सदाचारो ब्रह्मचर्यं वा Râmarshi and see note St. 82. कुरङ्गायते = मृगवद्भीतो भवति Râmarshi. But there is no propriety in भीतः. It is rather mildness that is meant here.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 110 “St. CX” ↓
r9St. CX.—लज्जागुणौघ = लज्जायाः ये गुणाः (commentary) I prefer the interpretation modesty and many other good qualities. The commentary in M. countenances this interpretation. अत्यन्तशुद्धहृदया and अनुवर्तमाना may be interpreted, I think, so as to go both with जननी and प्रतिज्ञा, the first as applied to mother meaning of very pure heart and the second always obediently following after one. As applied to प्रतिज्ञा, the former would mean that by which the heart becomes pure and the latter, always going with one, always binding. सुखमसूनपि संत्यजन्ति = either abandon comforts and even life, or abandon even life with ease i. e. without hesitation. For the former comp. Naishadha I., 60.

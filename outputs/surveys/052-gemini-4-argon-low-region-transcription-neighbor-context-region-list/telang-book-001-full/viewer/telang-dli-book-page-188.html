stanza_note 107 “St. CVII”
r4St. CVII.—On the second line Râmarshi says तस्य अर्धस्य पञ्चाशद्वर्षाणां परस्य अर्धस्य पञ्चविंशतिवर्षाणामित्यर्थः अर्धं सार्धद्वादशवर्षाणि पुनः सार्धद्वादशवर्षाणीत्यर्थः....शेषमुर्वरितमायुः पञ्चविंशतिपरिमितवर्षमित्यर्थः. This is not quite clear. I suggest the following : तस्य (आयुषः) परस्य (रात्रिगतार्धेतरस्य) अर्धस्य अपरमर्धम्. The commentary says ततोनन्तरं तस्य पञ्चाशत अ (sic)र्धस्य कदाचिदपि अधिकमर्धमपि बालत्ववृद्धत्वयोर्गतम्. According to our interpretation अपरम् would be used only to distinguish it from the first अर्धं but if so it might have been dispensed with. On बालत्व and वृद्धत्व Râmarshi says बाल्यमविवेकेनेत्यर्थः तथा वृद्धत्वं कार्यासक्त्या अवगणनयेत्यर्थः. व्याधिवियोगदुःख = the pains of illness and separation.— S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 108 “St. CVIII”
r5St. CVIII.—ब्रह्मज्ञानविवेकिनः = those who possess the discrimination (i.e. between things real and unreal) which results from knowledge of the Brahma. काञ्चन and धन together do not yield a good reading. The reading of R. is thus explained उपभोगमुपयोगं भजन्ते इत्येवंशीलानि (Râmarshi). भज् seems to mean here ‘affording.’ The meaning of the last two lines is clear but the construction is not so, a number of relatives having to be supplied. This Stanza occurs at p. 301 of the Kâvyasangraha.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
stanza_note 109 “St. CIX”
r6St. CIX.—परितर्जयन्ती—comp. Raghu IV., 28 and XI. 78., देहे is a better reading than देहम् as the locative is the case commonly employed with the root प्रहृ. अहितम् i. e. to oneself.—Vasantatilakâ.
stanza_note 110 “St. CX”
r7St. CX.—See Nîtis'ataka St. 92 note. पुरुषेषु रत्नं श्रेष्ठम्, according to Râmarshi, which agrees with our interpretation. अहह Râmarshi takes to be अद्भुते which will do as कष्टम् is खेदे.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

stanza_note 51 “St. LI”
r3St. LI.—स्वच्छन्दंम् is a better reading than स्वाच्छन्द्यम् as going with विहरणम्. As it stands we must take the two separately. अकार्पण्यमशनम् is one thing. कृपण—Nîtis'ataka St. 49. मनो. &c. comp. Gîtâ VI., 26. उदार = noble.—S'ikhariṇî.
stanza_note 52 “St. LII”
r4St. LII.—परिगत = लब्ध (Râmarshi.) I am not aware of any other use of this word in this sense, which does, however, seem to be the sense here. भैक्ष—see Pâṇini II., 4, 38. परिणतिः – comp. Vikramorvashî सेवाकाकुः परिणतिरभूत्. स्वात्मसंतोषिणः—Comp. Gîtâ III., 17 (and 19 as to निःसङ्गता), where S'ankara renders it by सर्वतो विगततृष्णः. व्यतिकर = contact. कर्म निर्मूलयन्ति—comp. St. 71. The Stanza also occurs at p. 316 of the Kâvyasangraha.—Sragdharâ.
stanza_note 53 “St. LIII”
r5St LIII.—तुरगचलचित्त is an unusual simile, but comp. उच्चैःश्रवसश्चञ्चलता Kâdambarî p. 208. पद = position, seat. The construction is जरा देहं हरति मृत्युः सकलमिदं जीवितं हरति as the commentator says. On तपस् see Nîtis'ataka St. 100.—S'ikhariṇî.
stanza_note 54 “St. LIV”
r6St. LIV.—यौवनलालना यौवनस्य तारुण्यस्य लालना धारणा (Râmarshi.) तारुण्यातिपालना says the other commentator. It probably means the happiness of youth; comp. Prasannarâghava लालयन्तमरविन्दवनानि (p. 55). धैर्यसमाधि = concentration and patience. निर्विकारचित्तस्य धारणम् (Râmarshi.) योगे = यमनियमासनप्राणायामप्रत्याहारध्यानध्येयधारणारूपे (Râmarshi.)—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

r7St. LV.—पाली = उपरिदेश (Râmarshi.) It is given as meaning edge in Amara and Medinî. कपाली = fragment of a pot.

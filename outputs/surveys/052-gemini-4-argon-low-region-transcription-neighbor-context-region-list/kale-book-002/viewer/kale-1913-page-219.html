stanza_note 11 “Sl. 11”
r3Sl. 11. नाम—Verily, forsooth; or ‘ may well be considered’. ग्राह—A shark. वितर्क—misgivings, doubts; अस्मिन्कृते इदं भविष्यति न भविष्यतीति चेत्यादयः ऊहाः Rámarshi. धैर्यद्रुमध्वंसिनी—felling the tree of courage on its bank. Cf. infra. सिकतिलनदीतीरतरुभिः Sl. 49. आवर्त—an eddy, a whirlpool. तटी—the bank of a river. The comp. should properly be तटीका; but it may be defended by the maxim समासान्तविधिरनित्यः. तस्याः पारगताः—those that have crossed it ( the river ) i. e. have conquered desire.
stanza_note 12 “Sl. 12”
r4Sl. 12. संसारोत्पन्नं चरितम्—The course of life in this world; incidents of worldly life. अनुपश्यामि—to expect something good out of, to see in prospect; cf. न च श्रेयोऽनुपश्यामि Bhag. I. 31. विपाकः —result. विमृशतः—to me when I reflect, when I take a right view of the matter. See Ni. S'a. 89. चिरपरिगृहीताः—( 1 ) enjoyed for a long time, or (2) enjoyed after a long time, as Rámarshi takes it. The former is, however, preferable, as long enjoyment is required to make a man attached to worldly pleasures which is implied in the succeeding line. महान्तो जायन्ते—become big; assume large proportions; hence tempt men by their magnitude. व्यसनं दातुमिव—their object in assuming big proportions and tempting men away from Moksha being as it were to make men unhappy. The more a man clings to pleasures the more miserable he becomes; for he is always disappointed; cf. न जातु कामः कामानामुपभोगेन शाम्यति । हविषा कृष्णवर्त्मेव भूय एवाभिवर्धते ॥ Manu II. 94. Or व्यसन may mean calamity; the fondness for enjoyments throws them into the turmoil of embodied existence; see com. and infra. Sl. 46. notes. विषयिणां–and not to विरागिणां who at once renounce them and become happy.

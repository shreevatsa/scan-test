r2MISCELLANEOUS.
translated_stanza 1 “Stanza 1”
r31. The sun is covered by day with the same strip of ambara ( sky ; cloth ) with which the moon is covered at night: oh the misery of these !
translated_stanza 2 “Stanza 2”
r42. When self-restraint developed by discrimination becomes manifest, and when the strong hold desire has upon man is relaxed, there appears that perfection ( the last stage of contemplation ) wherein even the lord of gods, becomes an object of pity, wretched (as he is under the influence of desire) on account of the strong distraction caused by the enjoyment of greatness worn out of age.
translated_stanza 3 “Stanza 3”
r53. Learning formerly served to remove the pain (of existence ) of those who possessed self-restraint. In course of time it came to be used for securing sensual happiness to sensualists. Seeing that the rulers of the earth are now averse to learning, alas ! it is even going down and down every day.
translated_stanza 4 “Stanza 4”
r64. The time (that was) happy on account of the enjoyment of lovely women is passed; and having, for long, wandered along this avenue of the world we are exhausted; now ( stationed ) on the bank of the Ganges (lit. the river of heaven ) we send forth (lit. extend) with deep sighs ( the cries of invocation with the words—S'iva, S'iva, S'iva.
translated_stanza 5 “Stanza 5”
r75. Mahàdeva is the god, and the stream of the gods (the Ganges ) alone is the stream (for me); caves are (my) abode, and even the quarters ( my ) raiment; the Destroyer is ( to me ) as a friend; and my vow—such conduct (as is ) free from meanness; or why say more (on this subject), let me be wedded to the Vata tree alone (lead a life of seclusion and piety ).

r447. Now truthful, now false; at one time harsh, at another speaking sweetly; now cruel and merciful afterwards; now parsimonious, but anon liberal; ever spending wealth and ever gaining large sums of money—thus the policy of kings, like a harlot, assumes a diversity of forms.

r649. Whatever amount of wealth, large or small, might have been allotted to ( lit. written on the forehead of ) a man, he certainly obtains even in the country called Maru; on the mountain Meru he will not be able to get more. Have patience then, and do not adopt a servile course of conduct towards the wealthy for nothing. See that a jar draws ( lit. takes up ) the same quantity of water from a well as well as from the sea.

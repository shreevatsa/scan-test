stanza_note 98 “Sl. 98”
r3Sl. 98. यदा—is equivalent here to यदि. दौषो &c—The spring is the season for the trees to put forth new leaves. करीर a thorny shrub growing in a desert; it is eaten by sheep and camels. उलूक—an owl. अपि stands for च. सूर्यस्य—सरति आकाशे सूर्यः । यद्वा सुवति कर्मणि लोकं प्रेरयति ( urges men to action ); from सू + र्य ( क्यप् कर्तरि; क्यपो रुट् ); it is irregularly derived by 'राजसूयसूर्य &c.' Pan. III. 1. 114. चातकमुखे—The bird चातक is supposed to drink only a few drops of rain water when the sun is in Svâti. Various discrepant stories are current about this bird with which every one is familiar. मेघः—मेहति सिञ्चतीति.

Kau. on Pán III. 1. 19. ननु—here used to introduce an objection—but, are they not &c. हतविधि—accursed fate. Cf. हतहृदयं S'ak. VI. 6. हतविधिलसितानां ही विचित्रो विपाकः । Sis XI. 64. The word दग्ध is also similarly used. See Vais. S'a. sl. 22. The words हतक is more commonly used in this sense but at the end of a comp.; न खलु विदितास्ते चाणक्यहतकेन । Mud. II. परिभूताः स्थ रामहतकेन । Utt. I. वशगाः—वशं गच्छन्तीति वशगाः । under the power of fate.

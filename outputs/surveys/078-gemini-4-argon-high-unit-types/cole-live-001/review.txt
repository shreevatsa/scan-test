Preserve exact mathematical symbols and notation, including minus-or-plus ('∓'), plus-or-minus ('±'), congruence ('≡'), partial derivative ('∂'), integral ('∫'), multiplication cross ('×'), raised dot used for prime factorization products (e.g., '23.53', '287.1323536760'), and superscripts ('2^{2^n} + 1', '2^{67} - 1', '4^+').
Preserve symbolic footnote markers ('*', '†') both inline in the main text and at the start of each footnote.

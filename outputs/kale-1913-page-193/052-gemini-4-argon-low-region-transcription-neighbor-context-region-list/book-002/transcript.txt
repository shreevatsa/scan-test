    stanza_note 66 [u2]
      r2: Sl. 66. महाशैल &c.—शिलानां संघातः शिलासंघातः । महांश्चासौ शैलश्च महाशैलः तस्य शिलासंघातः like a dense ( सं ) line ( घात ) of rocks &c. The fig. here is उपमा, वत् the simile-expressing aff. being omitted in the compound उत्पलकोमलम्. For a similar idea, see sl. 63.
    stanza_note 67 [u3]
      r3: Sl. 67. नामापि न श्रूयते—even its name is not heard i. e. no trace of it is left. ज्ञायते V. l. known i. e. through the medium of the ears. मुक्ताकारतया—by reason of its assuming the form of a pearl. स्वात्याम्–In Swâti i e. while the sun is in conjunction with the constellation Svâti. It is supposed that at the time of the sun coming in conjunction with this constellation the pearl-shells burst open and the rain-drops that they receive into their cavity form pearls; see Bṛhatsanmhitâ of Varâhamihira XV. 13. Cf. पात्रविशेषे न्यस्तं गुणान्तरं व्रजति शिल्पमाधातुः । जलमिव समुद्रशुक्तौ मुक्ताफलतां पयोदस्य ॥ Mâl. I. 6. सन्मौक्तिकम्—A good or faultless pearl. सत् is evidently preferable to तत्. जायते–Since this verb is repeated a strict rhetorician would detect in this कथितपददोष ( the fault of repeating a word with the same meaning ). The reading—जुषां &c. is free from this fault. The fig. is अर्थान्तरन्यास.
    stanza_note 68 [u4]
      r4: Sl. 68. Some take एव with भर्तुः. but that is not good; the construction should be यद्भर्तुर्हितमेवेच्छति तत्कलत्रम्–meaning ‘she that desires the good only of her husband deserves to be called ‘wife.’ कलत्रम् Mark the gender of this word which is neu.; the gender of Sanskrit words in some cases is quite arbitrary as remarked by Pân.– लिङ्गमशिष्यं लोकाश्रयत्वाल्लिङ्गस्य । Cf. the word दार which is masc., and is always used in the plural. पुण्यकृतः—पुण्यं कृतवान् पुण्यकृत्. The aff. क्विप् ( ०—त् ) is added to कृ after the words mentioned in the Sûtra सुकर्मपापमन्त्रपुण्येषु कृतः । Pân. III. 2. 89.

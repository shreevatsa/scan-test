    stanza_note 26 [u4]
      r4: Sl. 26. महाराम—आराम means a garden, but more probably here ‘delight, pleasure; his path is full of delight because he is  free from all desire. चर्मन्—( 1 ) bark; ( 2 ) skin. सुखैः &c.— Mr. Telang says—“The result is the same with what are called pleasures or with sorrows; i. e. whether I am in wordly affluence or otherwise, I get food and clothing in one case as in the other.” But सुख and दुःख here seem to refer to the two courses of life marked out, viz. that of happily living on alms and worshipping S’iva or that of living a miserable life of sycophany and humouring rich men. सदृश—should better be rendered by ‘similar’ than by ‘the same’. The result, viz. obtaining food and clothing is similar in both cases but not the same as the one is full of happiness, the other attended by misery. त्रिनेत्र, °महीभृत्—mark the contrast suggested by these words.

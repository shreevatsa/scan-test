    stanza_note 111 'St. CXI' [u2]
      r3: St. CXI.—लालाायते see Siddh. Kaum. II., 223 ओज शब्दो वृत्तिविषये तद्वति. I owe this explanation to Mr. Râjârâm S'âstrî.—S'ârdûlavikrîiḍta.
    stanza_note 112 'St. CXII' [u3]
      r4: St. CXII.—The adjectives in the first three lines go both with नरः and नटः. संसारान्ते i. e. at the end of this life. जवनिका= curtain. विशति with जवनिका must be taken as equivalent to ‘goes  behind.’—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 113 'St. CXIII' [u4]
      r5: St. CXIII.—स्त्रैण = collection of women. See Siddh. Kaum. I., 491 and comp. Uttararâmacharita p. 114. यान्तु perhaps is a better reading than यान्ति.—S'ikhariṇî.

    stanza_note 1 'St. I' [u6]
      r7: St. I.— अकिंचन = indigent comp. Kumâra. V., 77. समचेतसः see St. 84 supra. सुखमया is not grammatically correct सुखमय्यः, which is, breaks the metre.—Anushṭub.
    stanza_note 2 'St. II' [u7]
      r8:  St. II.—On the last two lines Râmarshi says “वा अथवा कियद्वक्ष्यामः अत्र वर्तमानार्थे भविष्यत्प्रयोगः कियद्वदामः बतेति खेदे त्वया आत्मनि स्वस्मिन् किमिव किमु न अपकृतं कः अपकारः न कृतः कथं यावत्साकल्येन तावत्प्रथमं त्वया पुनरपि भूयोपि तदेव आत्मनि अपकृतमेव व्यवसितं कर्तुमारब्धम्. R. also supplies हे हृदय at the beginning. It will be observed that यावत् and तावत् in R.’s explanation are not taken as correlatives. I think the translation should be thus : “ Alas ! what self-injury is there having done which to yourself you have not immediately worked at the same again !”—S'ikhariṇî. 
    stanza_note 3 'St. III' [u8]
      r9: St. III.—ग्राम = collection. अम्भोजचन्द्रिका comp. Prasannarâghava p. 159. विडम्बनम् = mockery. Nothing noble can withstand the claims of the stomach!—Hâriṇî.
    stanza_note 4 'St. IV' [u9]
      r10: St. IV.—Râmarshi supplies a चेत् with the first three lines, unnecessarily.—Anushṭub.

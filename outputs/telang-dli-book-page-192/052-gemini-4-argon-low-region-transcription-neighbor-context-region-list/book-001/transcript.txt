    stanza_note 18 'St. XVIII' (open start) [u2]
      r3: उभयोरपि पक्षयोरिहलोकपरलोकलक्षणयोः एकोपि न किंचित्—जातजराः = born and dead i. e. they do not really live. R. says उत्पन्नमात्रा मृतमात्रा इत्यर्थः निरर्थकावतारा इति भावः. In उपरिष्टात् and अधः there is also an oblique reference to the positions of ध्रुव and कूर्म (as suggested by Mr. A. V. Kâthavaṭe) and perhaps also to the पक्ष which should enable them to go upwards and downwards. उदुम्बरमशक is a proverbial expression like कूपमण्डूक.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita. 
    stanza_note 19 'St. XIX' [u3]
      r4: St. XIXIII.—निविशते = sticks obstinately to. See Siddh. Kaum.  II., 241. संन्यस्यति = abandons, becomes indifferent to.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.

    stanza_note 22 'St. XXII' [u6]
      r7: St. XXII.—सुखैर्वा &c. = the result is the same with [what are called] pleasures or with sorrows i. e. whether I am in worldly affluence or otherwise I get food and clothing in the one case as in the other. The contrast between त्रिनेत्र and अन्ध is to be noted.—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 23 'St. XXIII' [u7]
      r8: St. XXIII.—करटिनः = elephants ; for the first line comp. St. 89. निबद्धफलक = prepared board. See St. 39 (the reading भुवनफलके) but the explanation of निबद्ध is not satisfactory. For झणज्झांकार comp. Uttararâmacharita ; it means noisy. The last line is the same as in St. 48.—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 24 'St. XXIV' [u8]
      r9: St. XXIV.—The second line is fatalism. Comp. यदभावि न तद्भावि भावि चेन्न तदन्यथा ॥ असंकल्पयन् thinking about, forming anticipations about. गमागम = going and coming. अनुभवस्व must be taken as derived from भू प्राप्तौ Siddh. Kaum. II., 181.—Pṛithvî.

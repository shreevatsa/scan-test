    stanza_note 24 (open start) [u2]
      r2: to simple words, and not to compounds. Vâmana remarks-भवार्थे पुनर्दिगादिपाठेऽपि वंशशब्दस्य वंशशब्दादेव तत्र प्रत्ययः । ग्रहणवता प्रातिपदिकेन तदन्तविधेः प्रतिषेधात् । The form may be defended by explaining it as तुल्यकुले साधवः, यत् being affixed by ‘तत्र साधुः.’ Pân. IV. 4. 98.
    stanza_note 25 [u3]
      r3: Sl. 25. हिम॰—कणशीकर v. l.; here कण means a drop and शीकर spray. हिमवतः स्थानानि—retreats in the Himâlayas, the favourite haunts of ascetics. सावमान &c.—see com.; given with, accompanied by, indignities; or सावमानं यथा स्यात्तथा with humiliation. रताः—take pleasure in.
    stanza_note 26 [u4]
      r4: Sl. 26. कन्दर—the slopes between two hills; see sl. 79. प्रसभं—प्रगता सभा विचारोऽस्मात् तथाभू तथा । forcibly i. e. against one’s thoughts, or wishes. This may also be taken with अपगत॰. प्रश्रय—courtesy, courteous behaviour. ‘प्रश्रयप्रणयौ समौ’ Amara. The last line is adj. to मुखानि.— लतानि—This refers to the eyebrows of men proud of their wealth which are sometimes raised, at others wrinkled. Cf. Bhâg. II. 2-5. quoted in the com., reading the 3rd and 4th lines as रुद्धा गुहाः किमजितोऽवति नोपसन्नान् कस्माद्भजन्ति &c.
    stanza_note 27 [u5]
      r5: Sl 27. The reading प्रिय has been adopted in this ed. instead of प्रिये, as mas. vocatives often occur, while a female is rarely addressed in this S'ataka. प्रणयिनि violates the metre. प्रणयिनी—must be taken with इति some how. Another reading is प्रियेऽत्र सलिलैः; but this, too, is not satisfactory, as there is no propriety of प्रियेः. अकर्कश v. l.—not pleasing, harsh to the touch; rough. यामः is replaced by यावः which is found in one ed. ईश्वराणां—goes with ग्रामाणि, and means ‘of rich people’; cf. ( Mis. sl. 11.) वित्त &c.—विकाराः such as rudeness, vanity &c. Mr. Telang’s reading is वित्तव्याधि which is redundant as we have already got विवेकमुष्. For the idea cf. Mis. sl. 32.
    stanza_note 28 [u6]
      r6: Sl. 28. प्रतिवनम्—in every forest. अखेदम्—an adj. ( Bah com. ) or adv. For the idea expressed here comp. infra Sl. 55.
    stanza_note 29 (open end) [u7]
      r7: Sl. 29. वर्धन्ते—appear long. The man who has the misery to supplicate the rich, often in vain, finds the days very long. आवेग

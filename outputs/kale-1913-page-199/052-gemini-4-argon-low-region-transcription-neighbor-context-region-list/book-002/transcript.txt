    stanza_note 92 'Sl. 92.' [u4]
      r4: Sl. 92. फलं i. e. the good or bad result of their actions, happiness or misery. कर्मायत्तं—Cf. Sl.s. 99, 101, 102. Here there is a reference to the two of the three divisions of Karman viz. संचित, प्रारब्ध and क्रियमाण. सुधिया सुविचार्यैव कुर्वता भाव्यम = सुधिया सुविचार्यैव व्यवहारः कार्यः । The construction should be noted. When the potential pass. p. is used as predicate the noun or adjective coming after it ( as कुर्वता here ) must agree with the agent ( सुधिया here ).
    stanza_note 93 'Sl. 93.' [u5]
      r5: Sl. 93. खल्वाटः—a bald-headed man. सशब्दं—i. e. the fruit making a loud noise as it fell. “ The fact of the tree being ताल ( Palm ) adds to the force of the lines as it gives little or no shade” Telang. The fig. is अर्थान्तरन्यास.
    stanza_note 94 'Sl. 94.' [u6]
      r6: Sl. 94. ग्रहपीडनम्—trouble from ग्रह—viz. Ráhu. मतिमतां here the aff. मत् expresses प्राशस्त्य or excellence. Cf. the last line with लिखितमपि ललाटे प्रोज्झितुं कः समर्थः H. ‘In the Kâvyasangraha this stanza is given as Vetálabhatta’s p. 39.’ Mr. Telang’s note. The fig.s are अर्थान्तरन्यास and काव्यलिंग.

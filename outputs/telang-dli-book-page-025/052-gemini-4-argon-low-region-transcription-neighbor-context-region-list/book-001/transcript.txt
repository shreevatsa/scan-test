      r1: CRITICAL NOTICE.
    paragraph [u3]
      r2: IN preparing the text of the present edition of the Nîtis'ataka, altogether fifteen different copies have been consulted. Of these, six copies are printed, and the remaining nine are MSS. The printed copies are the following :—
    paragraph 'C.' [u4]
      r3: C. The Bombay Oblong edition, lithographed and accompanied by a commentary. I have generally followed its text.
    paragraph 'T.' [u5]
      r4: T. The Bombay edition accompanied by a translation into Marâṭhi. After I had prepared my text and Variæ lectiones, I found that there had been more than one edition with a translation into Marâṭhi printed. But I did not consider it either necessary or useful to consult these other editions.
    paragraph 'Bo.' [u6]
      r5: Bo. The edition of Bohlen. I have also made use of the Variæ lectiones e codicibus Chambersianis petitæ collected by A. Weber and appended at the close of Bohlen’s volume. These I have called ‘Bo.n.’
    paragraph 'Be.' [u7]
      r6: Be. The copy in Benfey’s Chrestomathie. I have also made use of the various readings. They are called ‘Be.n.’
    paragraph 'K.' [u8]
      r7: K. The copy in the Kâvyasangraha recently printed at Calcutta. This work seems to be very much like a reprint of Hæberlin’s anthology, and it will be found that, in the majority of cases at least, the reading in ‘K.’ and in Be.n. (which is from Hæberlin) coincide.
    paragraph 'N.' [u9]
      r8: N. A copy of the three S'atakas of Bhartṛihari printed in Telugu characters. This copy has been sent me by my friend Mr. V. N. Narasimmiengar of Bangalore who tells me that this  copy represents the recension universally current in the South.

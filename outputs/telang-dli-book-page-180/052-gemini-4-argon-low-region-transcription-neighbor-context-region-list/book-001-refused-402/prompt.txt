- shataka (a larger unit, made of other units; contains heading, section; may stand at the top of flow main): The Sanskrit text of one śataka, with its title and sections.
- section (a larger unit, made of other units; contains heading, lead_in, verse): A group of stanzas on one theme, with its heading, such as अथ दुर्जनप्रशंसा ।
- notes (a larger unit, made of other units; contains heading, stanza_note; may stand at the top of flow main): The English notes on the stanzas, after the text.

- paragraph (printed as regions): A paragraph of the English preface, introduction or critical notice, such as one describing an edition, C. The Bombay Oblong edition, …; or a line introducing corrections in the addenda et corrigenda.
- corrigendum (printed as regions): One correction of the addenda et corrigenda, such as P. 6. (Text) line 1 from bottom, for … read …, with its page reference.

- lead_in (printed as regions): A short Sanskrit line of its own that introduces the next stanza, such as साहंकारं पुरुषमुद्दिश्याह ।

Bo. M. An entry can run onto the next page; the apparatus at the top of that page then continues it without a numeral, for example from (c).
- stanza_note (printed as regions): The English note on one stanza, beginning with its number, such as St. XXXIII.—, and often ending with the name of the metre, such as —Anushṭubh. A note can run across pages.
- footnote (printed as regions; may stand at the top of flow notes): A footnote below the rule in the preface, introduction, critical notice or notes, marked *, †, ‡, §, ¶ or ‖; or a note printed below an index table, such as N. B.—The numbers marked * refer to the Miscellaneous Stanzas….

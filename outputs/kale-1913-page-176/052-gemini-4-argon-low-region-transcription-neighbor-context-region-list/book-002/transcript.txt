    stanza_note 19 'Sl. 19' [u3]
      r3: Sl. 19. केयूरः—an armlet. संस्कृता—polished or refined by culture. Cf. Kâli. संस्कारवत्येव गिरा मनीषी तया स पूतश्च विभूषितश्च’ Kum. I. 28, where Malli. renders the word संस्कार by व्याकरणजन्या शुद्धिः । Here of course the word means more than that. संतत—Lasting for ever.
    stanza_note 20 'Sl. 20' (open end) [u4]
      r4: Sl. 20. नाम—is used here प्राकाश्ये ‘as is well known’; or it may have the sense of ‘indeed, verily’ as in मया नाम जितं or न नाम शरासनम् Vikra. प्रच्छन्नगुप्त—The Comp. may be taken in two ways प्रच्छन्नं यथा तथा गुप्तं very carefully protected, or प्रच्छन्नमत एव गुप्तं च carefully concealed ( from view and protected ). Cf. हर्तुर्याति न गोचरं supra sl. 16. भोगकरी—contributing to pleasures. from भोग+कृ+टः ( अ ) added हेतौ. + ई; see com.; ट is attached to कृ in these senses:–यशस्करी विद्या, श्राद्धकरः ( ताच्छील्य ), वचनकरः ( आनुलोम्य ); Sid. Kau. गुरूणां गुरुः the preceptor of preceptors, or the greatest of the great. For the gen. गुरूणां see Pân II. 3. 41. ( यतश्च निर्धारणम् ) and A. G. § 82. The fem. of गुरु is गुर्वी or गुरुः । The rule is ‘वोतो गुणवचनात्’ । Pân. IV. 1. 44. खरुसंयोगोपधान्न । Vârt.–Adjectives of quality ending in उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant, except खरु, form their fem. by adding ई optionally. See Gr. § 319. बन्धु—बध्नाति मनः स्नेहादिनेति बन्धुः । बन्ध् + उ Un. I. 10. राजसु पूजिता—The construction is rather unidiomatic from a grammatical point of view. The rule of Pân. is ‘क्तस्य च वर्तमाने’ II. 3. 67. Past p. participles ending in त, when used in the sense of the present tense, are used with the gen.; राज्ञां मतः, बुद्धः, पूजितो वा. The Loc may be explained as विषयाधिकरणे, राजसु विषये पूजिता or the whole may be taken as a comp. word meaning well ( सु ) respected ( पूजिता ) by kings ( राजभिः ). विद्याविहीनः पशुः—Comp. sl.s 12, 13.

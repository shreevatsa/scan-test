    stanza_note 18 'St. XVIII' [u2]
      r3: St. XVIII.—पिठरजकपाल = a fragment produced from (i. e. of) a jar. Râmarshi says कपालं अर्पितं गले यस्य which is unauthorised. The usual method gives quite good enough sense—“whose neck is tied to a fragment of a pot.”—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 19 'St. XIX' [u3]
      r4: St. XIX.—Although the worldly enjoyments available are so poor, still the love of them is not destroyed. This Stanza occurs at p. 304 of the Kâvyasangraha.—Vasantatilakâ.
    stanza_note 20 'St. XX' [u4]
      r5:  St. XX.—कविजनानां श्रेष्ठकवीनां विशेषैर्विशेषवचनैः says Râmarshi, which is not correct. गुरुकृतम् = made much of. The first two comparisons are common. Comp. Das'arupâvaloka p. 193.—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 21 'St. XXI' [u5]
      r6: St. XXI.—दाहात्म्यम् seems a more appropriate reading here. माहात्म्यम् would mean simply greatness. On पततु Râmarshi says लिङर्थे लोट् पतेदिति संभावना ॥ जटिल = उद्बुद्ध. कामान् = विषयान् (Râmarshi.) Comp. न जातु कामः कामानामुपभोगेन शाम्यति. This Stanza also occurs at p. 302 of the Kâvyasangraha.—S'ikhariṇî.
    stanza_note 22 'St. XXII' [u6]
      r7: St. XXII.—फल is better than बिस as more commonly used in such places. See St. 93 for one instance. अनुमन्तुम् = अङ्गीकर्तुं सोढुम्. (Râmarshi.) Comp. Miscellaneous St. 28.—Mâlinî.
    stanza_note 23 'St. XXIII' [u7]
      r8: St. XXIII.—तृणं यथा = the more common expression would be तृ णमिव. This Stanza occurs at p. 308 of the Kâvyasangraha.—Hâriṇî.
    stanza_note 24 'St. XXIV' [u8]
      r9: St. XXIV.—उपासित &c. is a Dvandva compound the first member stopping at गुरु. मानद = महत् (Râmarshi.) The other commentator derives it from दो अवखण्डने; rightly, but wrongly analyses it thus मानं ददाति. एकान्ततः = immutably, decidedly.—S'ârdûlavikrîdita.
    stanza_note 25 'St. XXV' [u9]
      r10: St. XXV.—नृपशतैर्यातम्—comp. Stanza 5 इव—see St. 32 Nîtis'ataka. लेशे is somewhat remarkable though justifiable see Siddh. Kaum. I., 311 and note there by Prof. Târânâth. विषाद i. e. for having so little. प्रत्युत = on the contrary.—S'ikhariṇî.

    stanza_note 36 (open start) [u2]
      r2: contemplation. Or Samâdhi may simply mean a state of equanimity not allowing the mind to be ruffled or agitated under any circumstances. Some take the comp as धैर्यं च समाधिश्चताभ्यां, but without propriety. The reading ध्येयसमाधि presents no difficulty. Samâdhi is the last and the most perfect stage of meditation, the eight stages being यमनियमासनप्राणायामप्रत्याहारधारणाध्यानसमाधयोऽष्टाङ्गानि । विधध्वं—2nd. pers. pl Imper. of धा with वि. बुद्धिं विधध्वं—Form your inclination towards, fix your mind on. The figures are Upamâ and Kâvyalinga.

    stanza_note 38 [u4]
      r4: Sl. 38. कृच्छ्रेण—with great difficulty; modifies स्थीयते. तद्भूमिः— ऋतुभिः v. l.. गर्भवासे—°गर्ते v. l.. अमेध्य—unfit for sacrifice; hence impure, dirty. नियमित—regulated by boundaries; hence confined in a limited area; pent up. गर्भवासे—गर्भस्य वासः the womb. व्यतिकर—means सम्बन्ध or मिश्रण, and may be translated by on account of. उपभोगः—विप्रयोगः v. l. will not do, as it means the same thing as विश्लेष ( separation ) which has already occurred. नारीणाम् &c.—This is taken by some with the second line; it is, however, preferable to take the third line as one assertion, नारीणामवज्ञा being the reason why old age is an evil. Râmarshi supports this view. For construction see com. वामाक्षीणामवज्ञा° v. l.—अवज्ञया विहसितानि तेषां वसतिः.

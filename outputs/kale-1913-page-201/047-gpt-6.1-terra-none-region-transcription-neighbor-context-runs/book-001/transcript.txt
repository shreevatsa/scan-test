    stanza_note 97 (open start) [u2]
      r2: rshi; marked or settled as one’s measure i. e. portion destined to fall to one’s share. उपनमेत्—Lit. go to, fall to one’s lot. Comp. for this sense of नम् with उप, इदमुपनतमेवं रूपमक्लिष्टकान्ति S'ak V. 19. कस्यात्यन्तं सुखमुपनतं &c. Meg. Some read उपनयेत् supplying दैवं as the subject, but then the construction becomes clumsy. कारणम्—i. e. to obtain more. आशा—directions; there is also an indirect reference to the other meaning— ‘hopes, expectations’. वर्षति—Loc. of the pres. p. of वृष्, which, though trans., is here used intransitively as its object जल is well-known. Cf. धातोरर्थान्तरे वृत्तेर्धात्वर्थेनोपसंग्रहात् । प्रसिद्धेरविवक्षातः कर्मणोऽकर्मिका क्रिया ॥ Sid. Kau. द्वित्राः—A Bah. Comp.; संख्येये यो बहुव्रीहिस्तस्माद् डच् ( अ substituted for the final vowel ) स्यात् ।  Sid. Kau. on Pàn. V. 4. 73. See com. बहुव्रीहौ &c
    stanza_note 98 [u3]
      r3: Sl. 98. यदा—is equivalent here to यदि. दोषो &c—The spring is the season for the trees to put forth new leaves. करीर a thorny shrub growing in a desert; it is eaten by sheep and camels. उलूक—an owl. अपि stands for च. सूर्यस्य—सरति आकाशे सूर्यः । यद्वा सुवति कर्मणि लोकं प्रेरयति ( urges men to action ); from सू + यं (क्यप्  कर्तरि; क्यपो ह्रस्वः); it is irregularly derived by ‘राजसूयसूर्य &c.’ Pan. III. 1. 114. चातकमुखे—The bird चातक is supposed to drink only a few drops of rain water when the sun is in Sváti. Various discrepant stories are current about this bird with which every one is familiar. मेघः—मेहति सिञ्चतीति.
    stanza_note 99 (open end) [u4]
      r4: Sl. 99. नमस्यामः—A denominative, य ( क्यच् ) is added to नमः in the sense of ‘ paying homage to ’; नमस्यति देवान् । पूजयतीत्यर्थः । Sid. Kau. on Pán III. 1. 19. ननु—here used to introduce an objection—but, are they not &c. हतविधि—accursed fate. Cf. हतहृदयं S'ak. VI. 6. हतविधिलसितानां ही विचित्रो विपाकः । Sis XI, 64. The word दग्ध is also similarly used. See Vais. S'a. sl. 22. The words हतक is more commonly used in this sense but at the end of a comp.; न खलु विदितस्ते चाणक्यहतकेन । Mud. II. परिभूताः स्म रामहतकेन । Utt. I. वशगाः—वशं गच्छन्तीति वशगाः । under the power of fate. The gods, Indra included, are finite beings, whose existence as separate deities terminates at the end of a Kalpa

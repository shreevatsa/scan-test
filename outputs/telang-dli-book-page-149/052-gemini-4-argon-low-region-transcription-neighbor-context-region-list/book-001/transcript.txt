    stanza_note 88 'St. LXXXVIII' [u4]
      r5: St. LXXXVIII.—नेता. Some copies of the commentary give पुरोहित मन्त्री प्रधानः as renderings for this. Râmarshi has शिक्षकः पालयिता इत्यर्थः. Teacher or director seems to be the preferable rendering. प्रहरण = weapon. Compare the analogous forms चरण, श्रवण &c. ऐश्वर्यबल may be taken as either a Tatpurusha or a Dvandva compound. धिग्धिग्वृथापौरुषम् Râmarshi says पौरुषं बलं धिग्धिक् अस्तु यतः वृथा निरर्थकम्. This is not sufficiently close to the text. There are, I think, two ways of taking the words before us; धिक्धिक्  as an interjection and वृथा पौरुषम् as a separate assertion; or the whole thing as meaning “oh fie on abortive valour !”—S'ârdûlavikrîḍita.
    stanza_note 89 'St. LXXXIX' [u5]
      r6: St. LXXXIX.—कर्म = acts done in a past life comp. infra Stanzas 96, 99, and Vidvanmodatarangiṇî pp. 27, 28. कर्मानुरूपाणि शुभाशुभानि । प्राप्नोति सर्वोपि जनः फलानि ॥ आद्यन्तशून्येत्र जगत्प्रवाहे । क्रिया भवेत्कर्मत एव सर्वा ॥ कर्मापि पुंसां भवति क्रियातो । बीजाङ्कुरन्यायतया न दोषः ॥ सुविचार्य. The use of सु with such a purely verbal form is not common but may be observed now and then. Comp. Nâgânanda (Bomb. Ed.) p. 18 Sâhityadarpaṇa (Calc. Ed. 1869) p. 124.—Anushṭub.

    stanza_note 92 'St. XCII' [u8]
      r9: St. XCII.—तावत् = प्रथमम्. (commentary) I think सृजति तावत् means “does, indeed create but” &c. पुरुषरत्नम् may mean the jewel man, or a jewel of a man (i. e. a jewel-like man.) The former is preferable.—Drutavilambita.

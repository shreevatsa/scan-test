    stanza_note 3 [u3]
      r3: Sl. 3. अज्ञ—जानातीति ज्ञः ( ज्ञा+क ) न ज्ञः अज्ञः । सुखं, सुखतरं used as adverbs. विशेषं जानातीति विशेषज्ञः । ज्ञानलव०-लव m. is derived from लू to cut, to divide; a particle. दुर्विदग्ध-विदग्ध properly means what is well burnt, polished and burnished; hence refined with culture, learned; दुष्टं यथा तथा विदग्धः दुर्विदग्ध badly learned or wise, vain, arrogant ( with little learning; ज्ञानलवेन दुर्विदग्धः ). दुर्विदग्धो दुष्चतुरः अर्धज्वलित इत्यर्थः । Râmarshi. Cf. Little knowledge is a dangerous thing. ब्रह्मा-derived fr. बृह् 1. 6. P. to grow +मनिन्, the ऋ being changed to र् before it by Un. IV. 145. रंजयति—रंज् cau. means to colour, to impart one's hue to : hence to win over, to gratify or propitiate. The cau. form is रजयति when the meaning is to sport with or hunt deer; see Kir. VI. 24. Some read the line as ब्रह्मापि तं नरं &c. but this violates the laws of metre ( as it makes 17 mâtra’s in the Pâda instead of 15 or 18 ) and is therefore inadmissible.

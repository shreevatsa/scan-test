# 096-additive-contact-sheet-type-survey

A survey of more than twelve chosen PDF pages, sent as labelled contact sheets, proposes **additions** to a book's current type list. It is the request recipe of [091](../091-terra-contact-sheet-unit-types/README.md) and [092](../092-argon-low-contact-sheet-unit-types/README.md) (four by five cells of 256 × 384 pixels labelled with the PDF position, JPEG at quality 0.85, at most 16 sheets and 12 MB a request, larger surveys in batches, a five-minute deadline by the user's choice, strict structured output, 16,384 output tokens, store false) with the change of [095](../095-additive-page-image-type-survey/README.md): the request carries the book's current list and description and asks only for new types with where they belong and evidence pages, optional definition edits, possible duplicates among existing types as questions, and other questions. Two further changes from 091/092: the sheets show the chosen pages (not necessarily the whole book) in the order chosen, and the response has no page inventory.

Batches are checked one by one, as for 095, and combined: a name proposed by two batches keeps the first definition with the evidence pages of both; edits, duplicate groups and questions are deduplicated. Settings are in [config.json](config.json), the prompt for the draft types in [prompt.example.txt](prompt.example.txt), the response contract in [response-schema.example.json](response-schema.example.json). Saving, reuse, retry and pending proposals are as for 095, whose [snapshot](../095-additive-page-image-type-survey/snapshot/) holds the shared source.

Run [ramayanamanjari-001](../../outputs/surveys/096-additive-contact-sheet-type-survey/ramayanamanjari-001/) (2026-11-13): all 532 pages of the archive.org scan `ramayanamamjari00ksemuoft` as 27 sheets in two requests, Terra, $0.0881 ($0.0532 + $0.0349), 15 s of requests (about 100 s with rendering the sheets in the browser).
